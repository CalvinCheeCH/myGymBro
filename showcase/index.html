A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before the first set.

Does this demo connect to a real coach?+

This page uses scripted conversations and fictional records for Alex to show the product experience. It does not send messages to Telegram or read a real Notion workspace.

Can I change the coach's personality?+

Yes. Use /persona to list available voices or /persona batman to select one. Use /create to describe a custom tone, then activate it with /persona <id>. Personas change written delivery while retaining the coaching knowledge and instructions. Switching starts fresh chat context while your Notion records remain available.

How accurate are meal-photo numbers?+

Meal photos provide estimates. Portions, sauces, and preparation can change the numbers. The coach can ask for context and use ranges. Readable nutrition labels provide printed serving values that can be saved for reuse.

What do I need to use the product?+

A Telegram bot, a supported AI provider (Claude, Gemini, or OpenAI), a Notion integration, and a machine or hosting environment to run it. The current setup has one personal profile and one Notion workspace per deployment. Google Calendar and voice input are optional.

Can I use my existing program and everyday food?+

Yes. Training programs and references can be imported into the Knowledge Base. Describe the food you eat in English, including portions and preparation. The coach can ask for details and save repeat foods in your Food Library.

Where does my data go?+

The running product uses Telegram, Notion, and your selected AI provider. Optional features use their configured services, including Google Calendar and API transcription. Voice transcription can run locally. Weekly chart rendering uses QuickChart.

Can it replace a trainer or medical professional?+

It helps with fitness logging, planning, and coaching against your recorded information. Nutrition numbers are estimates, and health or injury concerns require a qualified professional.
